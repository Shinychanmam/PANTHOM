# PANTHOM

### Endless info, One AI.

PANTHOM is an AI platform designed to connect intelligence, specialized AI Packs, tools, creators, and enterprise infrastructure into one unified ecosystem.

Instead of using a separate AI system for every task, PANTHOM is built around a modular architecture where specialized capabilities can be loaded, executed, combined, and shared.

---

## Vision

> **Endless info, One AI.**

PANTHOM aims to make AI feel less like a single chatbot and more like an intelligent operating layer.

Users can discover specialized Packs, interact with AI systems, combine capabilities, and eventually create and publish their own Packs.

---

## Core Experience

PANTHOM is designed around an interactive experience rather than a traditional static website.

### AI Modes

* **Local** — Private, device-focused AI
* **Normal** — Connected AI experience
* **Enterprise** — Private infrastructure for organizations

### AI Packs

Specialized AI capabilities can be organized into Packs.

Initial concepts include:

* Mathematics
* Coding
* Aviation
* German
* Investing

More Packs can be added as the ecosystem grows.

---

## Platform

### Pack Engine

The Pack Engine is the foundation of the PANTHOM ecosystem.

It is designed to provide:

* Pack loading
* Pack execution
* AI ↔ Pack interaction
* Metadata
* Tool integration
* Testing
* Security
* Performance optimization

---

## Creator Ecosystem

PANTHOM is intended to allow creators and developers to build their own AI Packs.

A future creator workflow:

```text
NAME
  ↓
KNOWLEDGE
  ↓
TOOLS
  ↓
TEST
  ↓
PUBLISH
```

Creators can eventually distribute Packs through the PANTHOM Marketplace.

---

## Marketplace

The PANTHOM Marketplace is planned as a discovery and distribution layer for AI Packs.

Potential features include:

* Pack discovery
* Categories
* Search
* Pack details
* Creator profiles
* Ratings
* Reviews
* Reporting
* Free Packs
* Paid Packs
* Creator revenue sharing

PANTHOM is planned to retain a **20% platform fee** from marketplace transactions.

---

## Enterprise

PANTHOM is designed to eventually support organizations that require greater control over AI infrastructure.

Planned capabilities include:

* Private AI
* Offline deployment
* Enterprise servers
* Access control
* Security policies
* Audit logs
* Monitoring
* Enterprise data integration
* Private infrastructure
